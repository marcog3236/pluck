## Description
PLUCK is a unique trick-taking card game with a twist — before each hand, you pluck cards from your opponents!

**How it works:**
• 3 players compete to win tricks and meet their quota
• Before play begins, pluck a card from an opponent — give them one of yours and receive their highest card of that suit back
• The dealer chooses the trump suit each hand
• First to 10 points wins!

**Features:**
• Play solo against AI opponents (Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty)
• Online multiplayer with friends — create or join rooms with a 6-character code
• Beautiful dark-themed card table
• Smooth card animations
• Smart AI that adapts to difficulty level — from casual to ruthless card-counting

**Perfect for:**
• Card game enthusiasts looking for something fresh
• Quick games on the go (5-10 minutes per game)
• Playing with friends online

PLUCK takes the best of classic trick-taking games and adds the strategic "plucking" mechanic that changes everything. Every hand is different!
