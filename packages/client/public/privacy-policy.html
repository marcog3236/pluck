PLUCK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

PLUCK is a card game that respects your privacy. We collect minimal data and never sell your information.

Data We Collect

  • Player name: The display name you enter when starting a game. This is stored only in your device's memory during gameplay and is not persisted or transmitted to any server beyond the game session.
  • Multiplayer sessions: When playing online multiplayer, your display name and game actions are shared with other players in your game room via our game server. No account or personal information is required.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No email addresses, phone numbers, or real names
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No cookies

Third-Party Services

PLUCK does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

PLUCK does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The game does not require any personal information to play.

Data Retention

Game session data (player names, game state) exists only during active gameplay and is discarded when the session ends. No data is permanently stored on our servers.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
